Compliance & standards

Built for the DDAWS mandate.

The CMVR (Sixth Amendment) Rules, 2025 make Driver Drowsiness & Attention Warning Systems compulsory on buses and trucks. Blinksmart™ is built for it, and retrofits existing fleets.

1 Oct 2026School buses: camera and monitoring fitment (Rule 125C)
1 Jan 2027Braking, ABS & AEBS on new models
1 Oct 2027DDAWS, BSIS, MOIS, LDWS on new bus and truck models
1 Jan 2028DDAWS, BSIS, MOIS, LDWS on existing models

Source: G.S.R. 834(E), MoRTH, 11 Nov 2025.
