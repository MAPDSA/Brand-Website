FAQs

Questions before you join?

What exactly does BlinkSmart Home do?

A small camera watches the driver, not the road. When eyes close for too long, attention leaves the road, the phone comes out, or the seat belt is off, it alerts the driver inside the car right away. The family app tells the people you choose about trips, safe arrivals and serious alerts.

Does it record my family?

Alerts are worked out inside the car. Video is shared only if the owner chooses, and your data is stored in India. Everyone who drives the car is told about BlinkSmart at setup, and drivers can see their own score.

How is it different from a dashcam?

A dashcam records the road so you have evidence after a crash. BlinkSmart watches the driver and alerts them before a tired or distracted moment becomes a crash. You can use both.

Will it fit my car?

The pilot covers hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs and electric cars. Tell us your make and model when you join, and our team confirms the fit before the visit.

Who fits it, and where?

A trained BlinkSmart installer fits it in one visit, at your home or at a partner workshop in Bengaluru. They set up the family app on your phone and show the driver how alerts work.

How much does it cost?

You choose Own, Subscribe or a Road-trip pass. Pilot prices are shared with you on the call, before anything is fitted. Joining the list is free and you can say no at any point.

I live outside Bengaluru. Can I join?

The pilot is for Bengaluru pincodes for now. Leave your details anyway and we'll tell you when BlinkSmart Home reaches your city.

What if something stops working?

Call or WhatsApp the helpline. If the device needs repair or replacement, we swap it at your door or at a partner workshop.
